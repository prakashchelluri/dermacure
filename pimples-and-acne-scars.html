Pimples, pimple marks and acne scars.

Pimples can usually be controlled with the right medicines, and marks and scars can be improved over time with steady treatment.

Book for this

What it is

In plain words.

Pimples form when oil and dead skin block the small openings of the skin, and germs and swelling follow. After a pimple heals it can leave a mark, which is a change in colour, or a scar, which is a dip in the skin. Marks and scars are different problems and are treated differently.

You might notice

  • Blackheads, whiteheads, red bumps or pus-filled pimples on the face, chest or back.
  • Deep, painful lumps under the skin. In adults these are often along the jaw and chin.
  • Flat red or brown marks where pimples have healed. The skin feels smooth.
  • Small dips or pits in the skin that show more in side light.

Why it happens.

01

Hormones and oily skin

Hormones make the oil glands more active, which is why pimples often start in the teens. In adult women, pimples along the jaw and chin that flare before periods can point to a hormonal cause.

02

Steroid creams on the face

Creams that contain steroids, often sold as fairness or mixed creams, can cause a crop of small, similar-looking pimples and make the skin thin and red. Pimples often flare when the cream is stopped, so stopping needs a doctor's guidance.

03

Picking and late treatment

Squeezing or picking pimples can push the swelling deeper and raises the chance of marks and scars. Deep pimples left untreated for a long time are a common reason scars form.

At the clinic

What gets checked first.

The dermatologist first looks at the type of pimples you have, how deep they are, and whether you have marks, scars or both. You will be asked about your periods, hair growth on the face, the creams you have used, and any medicines or supplements you take. If you have used a steroid or fairness cream, or if you are pregnant or planning a pregnancy, say so, because it changes the treatment.

What treatment can involve

Prescription medicines and creams

What it isCreams such as benzoyl peroxide and retinoid creams, and tablets where needed, used as prescribed to control active pimples.

What to expectIt usually takes 2 to 3 months to see a clear change, and some creams cause dryness or mild irritation in the first few weeks.

Chemical peels

What it isA peeling liquid is applied to the skin so the top layer sheds in a controlled way, which helps with blocked pores and pimple marks.

What to expectPeels are done over several sittings a few weeks apart, and they help marks more than they help deep scars.

Microneedling with radiofrequency (MNRF)

What it isFine needles deliver heat into the deeper skin to encourage new collagen, which helps raise dipped scars.

What to expectIt takes several sittings spaced weeks apart, the result builds slowly over months, and scars can become shallower but do not disappear fully.

Vampire facial (microneedling with PRP)

What it isMicroneedling done along with PRP prepared from your own blood, used for shallow acne scars.

What to expectResults vary from person to person, more than one sitting is needed, and it suits shallow scars more than deep, narrow ones.

Straight talk

Pimples usually take 2 to 3 months of regular treatment to settle, and many people need a maintenance cream after that to keep them from returning. Marks fade slowly over many months, deeper ones can take a year or more, and sunscreen and treatment help them fade. Scars can be made shallower over several sittings, but treatment does not bring the skin back to exactly how it was, and new pimples can leave new scars.

What helps at home

  • Wash your face twice a day with a gentle face wash. Scrubbing hard can make pimples worse.
  • Do not squeeze or pick pimples.
  • Use sunscreen daily. Sun makes pimple marks darker and slower to fade.
  • Avoid heavy oils, thick creams, and any medicated cream from a friend or a shop without a prescription.

When to come in soon

See a dermatologist early if you have deep, painful pimples, if pimples are leaving marks or dips, or if they started after using a fairness or steroid cream. Preventing scars is easier than treating them.

Questions people ask.

Will my pimples come back after treatment?

They can. Pimples tend to come and go for some years, which is why a maintenance cream is often advised after they settle. If they return, treatment can be started again.

What is the difference between a pimple mark and a scar?

A mark is a flat change in colour, red or brown, and the skin feels smooth. A scar is a dip or pit that you can feel or see in side light. Marks usually fade with time and creams, while scars usually need procedures.

Can acne scars be removed completely?

No treatment can promise that. Scars can be made shallower and less visible over several sittings, and how much they improve depends on the type and depth of the scar.

I used a cream from the medical shop and my pimples got worse. What should I do?

Some shop creams, often mixed creams, contain steroids, which can cause pimples and thin the skin. Bring the tube to your visit and do not start another cream on your own. The dermatologist will guide you on stopping it, as the skin can flare when a steroid cream is stopped.
