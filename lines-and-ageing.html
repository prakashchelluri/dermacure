Fine lines, wrinkles and ageing skin.

Lines come from movement, loss of volume and sun, and each one needs a different kind of care.

Book for this

What it is

In plain words.

With age, skin gets thinner and drier, and it makes less collagen. Lines form where the face moves, the cheeks and under-eyes lose fullness, and sun adds dark spots and rough texture. This is normal, and how much you treat is your choice.

You might notice

  • Lines on the forehead, between the eyebrows and at the corners of the eyes, at first only when you smile or frown.
  • Hollow under-eyes, flatter cheeks and deeper folds from nose to mouth.
  • Dark spots, uneven tone and rough, dry skin on sun-exposed areas.
  • Skin that feels looser along the jawline and neck.

Why it happens.

01

Muscle movement

Years of smiling, frowning and raising the eyebrows fold the skin in the same places. In time these lines stay even when the face is at rest.

02

Loss of volume

With age the face loses fat and collagen, and the bone beneath also thins. Cheeks flatten, and folds and hollows look deeper.

03

Sun and smoking

Daily sun breaks down collagen and causes dark spots, fine lines and rough skin. Smoking adds to this damage.

At the clinic

What gets checked first.

The dermatologist looks at your face at rest and while you smile and frown, to see which lines come from movement and which from volume loss or sun. You will be asked about sun exposure, smoking, medicines, earlier treatments, and pregnancy or breastfeeding. Say what bothers you most, so the plan stays small and suited to you.

What treatment can involve

Prescription medicines and creams

What it isSunscreen, moisturiser and retinoid creams as prescribed are used to improve fine lines and sun damage.

What to expectChanges show slowly over three to six months, retinoid creams can cause dryness and peeling at first, and they are not used in pregnancy.

Botox

What it isSmall injections relax the muscles behind movement lines, such as frown lines, forehead lines and lines at the corners of the eyes.

What to expectThe effect shows in about 1 to 2 weeks and lasts about 3 to 4 months, then the lines slowly return, and it does not fill hollows or lift loose skin.

Dermal fillers

What it isA gel, most often hyaluronic acid, is injected to replace lost volume in the cheeks, folds, under-eyes or lips.

What to expectThe result shows at once, with some swelling or bruising for a few days, and often lasts about 6 to 18 months depending on the product and area.

PDO thread lift

What it isFine threads that dissolve on their own are placed under the skin to give a mild lift to the cheeks, jawline or brows.

What to expectThe lift is mild and wears off as the threads dissolve, often within a year, and it does not match surgery for very loose skin.

Chemical peels

What it isA mild acid is applied to remove the dull top layer of skin, which can help texture, tan, dark spots and very fine lines.

What to expectPeels need several sittings a few weeks apart, do nothing for deep lines or lost volume, and the result holds only with daily sunscreen.

Straight talk

Creams and peels work slowly over months, and Botox, fillers and threads wear off, so they need repeating if you want to keep the effect. No treatment stops ageing. The aim is a natural look, where you still look like yourself.

What helps at home

  • Use a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, and again if you are outdoors for long.
  • Moisturise daily and wash with a mild cleanser.
  • Do not smoke, and get enough sleep.
  • Avoid steroid "fairness" creams, because long use thins the skin and can cause redness and pimples.

When to come in soon

See a dermatologist soon if a spot on sun-exposed skin grows, bleeds, crusts or does not heal. After any injection treatment, get medical help at once if you have strong pain, skin turning pale or dusky, or any change in vision.

Questions people ask.

Will Botox make my face look frozen?

Small, well-placed amounts usually soften lines and still let your face move. The aim is a natural look. If you do not like the result, it wears off in a few months.

What is the difference between Botox and fillers?

Botox relaxes muscles, so it suits lines caused by movement, like frown lines. Fillers add volume, so they suit hollows and deep folds. You may be advised one, both or neither.

How long do the results last?

Botox shows in about 1 to 2 weeks and lasts about 3 to 4 months. Fillers often last 6 to 18 months, and threads often wear off within a year. Peels and creams help only while you continue them and use sunscreen.

What side effects can happen?

Redness, swelling and small bruises are common and usually settle within one to two weeks. Less often there can be a drooping eyelid for a few weeks after Botox, lumps after fillers or dimpling after threads, and rarely a filler can block a blood vessel and harm the skin or eyesight. Tell the doctor if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or taking blood thinners.
