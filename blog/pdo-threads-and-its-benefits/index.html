PDO threads and its benefits

10 February 2024

Thread Lifts represent an innovative cosmetic procedure employing FDA Approved
polydioxanone (PDO Threads) sutures, a material safely used in surgeries for many years.
These threads facilitate a non-surgical approach to lift, tighten, and rejuvenate facial skin,
offering a smoother and firmer appearance.
By harnessing PDO Threads, this technique stimulates collagen production, promoting natural
skin revitalization. This allows skilled professionals to reposition sagging skin, yielding
immediate improvements that continue to enhance over time. Remarkably, the results endure
for years, devoid of scarring, making this procedure an appealing " injectables " alternative to
surgery and various other rejuvenation methods.
This cutting-edge treatment has gained popularity as one of the most sought-after options in
cosmetic surgery, offering swift outcomes without significant downtime. In just around an hour,
individuals can experience a noticeable enhancement in their facial appearance, benefiting from
enduring results without enduring any scarring.

What are the Components of a PDO THREAD LIFT?

A PDO Thread Lift encompasses several essential components for its successful execution. At
its core are PDO Threads, crafted from a biodegradable polymer, which serve as the primary
agents for lifting and tightening the skin. These threads come in various types, including mono,
cog, and screw threads, each designed for specific rejuvenation purposes. The procedure
involves specialized insertion needles or cannulas to delicately place these threads under the
skin. To ensure patient comfort, local anesthesia is administered in the treatment areas.
Marking the designated treatment zones on the face or neck precedes the procedure, ensuring
precise placement of the threads for optimal results. Maintaining a sterile environment and
equipment is pivotal throughout the process to prevent infection. Aftercare instructions,
addressing post-procedure care and potential side effects, are provided to patients. Follow-up
appointments may also be scheduled to monitor healing progress and evaluate the
effectiveness of the lift. The harmonious integration of these components is vital to achieving the
desired outcomes of a PDO Thread Lift.

How Do They Work?

Dermacure Clinic in Rajahmundry harnesses the potential of PDO Threads to catalyze collagen
production in targeted areas, ensuring a natural and sustained volumizing lift. As these threads
gradually dissolve within the body, the stimulated collagen remains intact, preserving the
achieved volume and lasting lift.
At derma Clinic, our proficient injectors meticulously curate the ideal combination of threads and
employ precise techniques for their strategic placement. These threads are expertly positioned
to lift and contour the skin in a directional or criss-cross manner, effectively addressing various
signs of aging. Experience Immediate Rejuvenation with visible reduction in fine lines and
wrinkles right after the treatment, as PDO threads instantly lift and smoothen the treated areas.
Moreover, our approach ensures Long-term Collagen Enhancement as the skin continues its
natural healing process, generating fresh, rejuvenating collagen in the following months. This
revitalizes the skin, promoting a more supple, resilient, and youthful appearance that endures
even after the threads have been safely absorbed by the body.

What Can PDO THREAD LIFT Be Used For?

PDO thread lift treatment stands out as a sought-after, non-surgical option, specifically designed
for tightening and firming loose skin on the face, neck, and various body areas, thus bypassing
the need for surgical procedures. This multifaceted technique addresses a wide range of areas
including cheeks, eyebrows, jawline, neck, nasolabial folds, marionette lines, as well as the
arms, thighs, and abdomen, making it a comprehensive solution offered through PDO therapy.
The essence of this treatment lies in its ability to kickstart collagen production, thereby
catalyzing the skin's natural rejuvenation process. Remarkably, within about three months, the
newly synthesized collagen forms around the threads, creating a lasting lifting effect that can
persist for 12 to 18 months. Dermacure Clinic is dedicated to providing this innovative and
effective PDO thread lift treatment, ensuring clients receive a top-tier, non-invasive option for
their skin tightening and rejuvenation needs.

What is the Process For This Treatment?

The PDO thread lift treatment, being a non-invasive procedure, starts with the application of
both a topical numbing cream and a local anesthetic at the target area to ensure patient
comfort. The needles employed in this procedure are notably fine, which greatly minimizes any
discomfort. During the procedure, PDO threads are carefully introduced into the skin using
either cannulas or blunt-tipped needles.
These threads are strategically placed into the subcutaneous layer of the skin, where they serve
as supportive anchors during the lifting process. After the skin is effectively lifted, any protruding
parts of the threads are trimmed. This technique provides an immediate lifting effect by
tightening the underlying fat tissue. The PDO threads function analogously to a dermal filler, but
instead of depositing a filling substance under the skin, they leave a bio-compatible thread that
will gradually dissolve over time.

What are the Benefits of PDO THREAD LIFT


Non-Surgical Approach: This procedure provides a non-surgical alternative to traditional
facelifts or body lifting surgeries, reducing risks associated with invasive surgeries.
Skin Tightening and Lifting: PDO threads effectively lift and tighten sagging skin, providing an
immediate visible improvement in the treated areas.
Collagen Stimulation: By stimulating collagen production, PDO threads contribute to long-term
skin rejuvenation, enhancing skin texture, elasticity, and firmness.
Versatility: The treatment is versatile and can be applied to various areas of the face, neck,
and body, targeting multiple concerns such as sagging cheeks, jawline, necklines, and more.
Minimized Downtime: Compared to surgical procedures, PDO thread lifts generally involve
minimal downtime, allowing individuals to resume their daily activities relatively quickly.
Natural Results: Results from PDO thread lifts are natural-looking, providing subtle yet
noticeable enhancements without drastic changes in appearance.
Longevity: While individual results may vary, the effects of a PDO thread lift can last between
12 to 18 months, contributing to a lasting improvement in the treated areas.
Safety Profile: PDO threads are made from a biocompatible material that has been used in
various medical applications for years, exhibiting a good safety profile when administered by a
trained professional.
No Scarring: Unlike traditional surgical procedures, PDO thread lifts typically do not leave
visible scars since they involve minimal incisions.
Customizable: The procedure can be tailored to suit individual needs, allowing for a
personalized treatment approach based on specific aesthetic goals.

How long does it take a PDO THREAD LIFT take ?
The PDO thread lift procedure time is about 45-60 minutes depending on the treatment area
and it is usually conducted with a local anaesthetic. The application of the numbing cream and
prepping the skin can take up to 30 minutes whereas the procedure itself takes about 15-30
minutes.

Will I need More than one Treatment?
it's understood that sometimes a single PDO lift procedure may not suffice to meet the desired
aesthetic goals, leading to the possibility of additional treatments. After undergoing the initial
PDO lift, patients are advised to return to the clinic for a follow-up appointment, typically
scheduled between six to nine months post-procedure. This visit allows Dr. Sahitya and the
team to evaluate the results and determine if a second treatment is warranted.
In most cases, following this potential second session, further treatments are often not required
for about three years. The longevity of the results from PDO thread lifts, as generally extends
over a significant period, showcasing the enduring impact of this innovative cosmetic procedure.
