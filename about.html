The doctor

Dr. Sahitya Reddy

Consultant Dermatologist

Dr. Sahitya Reddy is the Consultant Dermatologist at Dr. Sahitya's Dermacure. She earned her MD in Dermatology, Venereology and Leprosy from Guntur Medical College.

She is a member of the Indian Association of Dermatology, Venereology and Leprosy, and has trained in cosmetic dermatology, trichology, non-surgical facelift, Botox and fillers.

Her areas of interest include acne treatment, acne scar remodelling, chemical peels, laser pigment and tattoo removal, non-surgical facelifts and Botox.

How she works.

01

She listens first

You say what is bothering you, how long it has been there, and what you have tried. Nothing is decided before that.

02

She explains plainly

You hear what the problem is and what each option involves, including what it will not do and whether it can come back.

03

You choose

The options are laid out and the decision is yours. Follow-up visits check how you are doing.
